Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026. Bills News Consolidated is an independent, one-person fan publication. This page explains, in plain language, what data this site touches and what it never touches.

What we collect

Analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and page views. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other sites.

Email. If you subscribe to The BNC Blitz newsletter, your email address is stored by MailerLite, our email provider, and used only to send you the newsletter. Every email carries an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from the list.

Advertising cookies. When Google AdSense ads run on this site, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com) or aboutads.info.

Affiliate links. Some links on this site are affiliate links (Amazon Associates, SeatGeek, Kalshi). If you click one and buy or sign up, the partner may use a cookie or referral code to credit us with a commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate, Bills News Consolidated earns from qualifying purchases.

What we never do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not collect names, addresses, payment details, or passwords on this site — purchases and sign-ups happen on the partner's own site, under their privacy policy. We do not run comment accounts or require registration to read articles.
