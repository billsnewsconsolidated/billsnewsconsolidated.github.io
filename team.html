Meet the Team

Five people, five desks, one team to cover. Every column on this site carries a name — this is who is accountable for what you read.

Front Office

Matt Manzella
Owner & Founder

Matt's first Bills game was Jim Kelly's last playoff game — a loss to the Jaguars. The adults around him, faces painted, were screaming that the dynasty was over. His passion for the Bills was just getting started.

He watched the Music City Miracle on TV, stayed die-hard through the whole 17-year playoff drought until Kyle Williams and Micah Hyde helped break it, and followed Josh Allen before he was famous — even driving from Buffalo to Allen's hometown of Firebaugh, California. He has flown his drone over the new Bills stadium and covered this team for years, starting with clips on Instagram before bringing that audience to Facebook and YouTube.

Today he runs Bills News Consolidated as a daily Bills operation — a YouTube channel with 6.2K subscribers, a Facebook community, and now this site. He writes the News desk and The Owner's Take, the morning Daily Links, and the trade and roster analysis — and his standing rule runs the whole building: if we can't verify it, we don't publish it.
